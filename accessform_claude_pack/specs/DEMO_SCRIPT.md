# 90–120 Second Demo Script

## Setup
Open `/` on projector. Keep `/live` available in another tab if phone audio is being used.

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem
“Hospital financial assistance may exist, but finding the right program and completing a multi-page form can be a major barrier if you are blind or low vision. AccessForm turns that paperwork into one conversation.”

Click/start call.

## 0:15–0:35 — Intent + discovery
Say: “I have a $7,800 bill from Cedars-Sinai and I can't afford it.”

Let `/live` visibly show:
- SerpApi searching official sources;
- HCAI/Cedars verified;
- current application found;
- Nutrient form extraction started/completed.

## 0:35–1:05 — Conversational form
Use the demo answers:
- lives alone;
- retired;
- Medicare;
- Social Security $2,050/month;
- no other income;
- proof of income is not available right now.

The application progress should update after every answer.

## 1:05–1:25 — Intelligence moment
AccessForm says the form fields are complete but proof of income is missing.

Show Xano completeness state:
- required fields complete;
- proof of income missing;
- signature pending.

## 1:25–1:45 — Payoff
Nutrient fills the real official PDF and runs accessibility processing.
Navigate to `/review`.

Show the actual filled application alongside:
“26 fields complete; one supporting document remains.”

## Closing line
“Today, AccessForm supports one hospital financial-assistance form end-to-end. The infrastructure can turn thousands of inaccessible or burdensome forms into conversations, while preserving the official document and human review.”
